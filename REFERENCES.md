# NASA data sources — FlameAtlas dataset v1.3.0

Every sourced value in `data/dataset.json` cites one of the entries below by its `sourceId`.
Per-run verification status is tracked in `eval/verification.md`.

## Primary NASA / NTRS sources

- **Saffire: A Novel Approach to Study of Spacecraft Fire Safety Using Un-manned Spacecraft** — NTRS 20180005168 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20180005168 (`S-SAF-IIII`)
- **Preliminary Results from the Saffire VI Experiment** — NTRS 20240002981 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240002981 (`S-SAF-IVVI`)
- **Fire Safety Implications of Preliminary Results from Saffire IV and V (ICES 2021)** — NTRS 20210017780 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210017780 (`S-SAF-IVV`)
- **Concurrent flame growth, spread and extinction over composite cotton-fiberglass fabric blend in microgravity** — NTRS 20150008962 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150008962 (`S-BASS2`)
- **Combustion of Solids in Microgravity: Results from the BASS-II Experiment** — NTRS 20160000593 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20160000593 (`S-BASS2b`)
- **Thickness and Fuel Preheating Effects on Material Flammability in Microgravity from the BASS Experiment** — NTRS 20140011099 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20140011099 (`S-BASS-SR`)
- **Saffire I-III Overview (sample-size comparison)** — NTRS 20170002628 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20170002628 (`S-SAF-CMP`)
- **Flammability of Materials on the Moon (FM2), Zayac et al.** — NTRS 20240015307 — https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20240015307 (`S-FM2`)
- **Flame spread over PMMA in microgravity, opposed low-speed flow (Scientific Reports)** — https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-017-18398-4 (`S-PMMA`)

## Dataset repository

- **NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) repository** — https://psi.nasa.gov/ (`PSI`). Source of the investigation catalogue and the documented operating ranges; each investigation page is `https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/<PSI-id>`.
