# Landing "spacecraft window" credits

The landing hero shows real NASA photographs in flight order. Files here are 16:9 WebP crops
(960/1600/2400 px) of each image's `~orig.jpg` from the NASA Image and Video Library. IDs, titles,
centres and dates come from the API records. The other frames reuse `/scenes/` files (see
`../scenes/CREDITS.md`): `iss030e075182` (low Earth orbit), `as17-134-20435` (on the Moon) and
`PIA24937` (Mars). These are scene-setting views, never evidence.

| File | NASA ID | Title (NASA) | Centre, date | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `depart-*.webp` | jsc2022e044289 | Orion Looks Back at Earth | JSC, 2014-12-05 | "A camera in the window of NASA's Orion spacecraft", Exploration Flight Test-1. Original is 946 px wide, so larger sizes are upscaled. |
| `outbound-*.webp` | art001e000475 | Flight Day 9: Orion Looks Back at Earth | JSC, 2022-11-24 | Artemis I, camera on a solar array |
| `approach-*.webp` | art001e000269 | Orion Approaches Moon for Outbound Powered Flyby | JSC, 2022-11-21 | Artemis I, camera on a solar-array tip |
| `flyby-*.webp` | art001e002594 | Flight Day 20: Lunar Close-up | JSC, 2022-12-05 | Artemis I, optical navigation camera |

Processing: cropped to 16:9 (no other edits), resized with Lanczos, WebP quality 74.
